FOR BHAVYA — SORRY WEBSITE

FILES
- index.html  : page content and your letter
- style.css   : layout, colours, responsive styling and romantic visuals
- script.js   : floating hearts and button interaction
- images/     : put your four photos here

ADD YOUR PHOTOS
Copy your four pictures into the images folder and rename them exactly:
  photo1.jpg
  photo2.jpg
  photo3.jpg
  photo4.jpg

If your photos are PNG or WEBP, either convert them to JPG or update the matching image src in index.html to the real extension.

IMPORTANT
In index.html, replace:
  [replace this with your honest reason before sharing]
with your own honest explanation before you send the page to Bhavya.

PREVIEW
Double-click index.html to open it in a browser. Internet is needed for the optional Google Fonts; the site itself works without them.

PUBLISH FREE
You can upload the folder to GitHub Pages or Netlify. Keep index.html, style.css, script.js and the images folder together.
